# arXiv preparation workspace

This directory is the staging area for a future ΛLingua preprint.

Nothing here should be treated as a submitted or final paper yet. The goal is to keep the manuscript, metadata, references, figures, and submission notes ready while the experiments are still being completed.

## Current state

- Experiment Zero: completed baseline and archived outputs.
- Phi replication: completed.
- Experiment One: design frozen; official acquisition run pending.
- Agent-Wire relay study: planned.
- arXiv submission: not started.

## Files

- `main.tex` — manuscript skeleton.
- `references.bib` — bibliography placeholder.
- `submission-metadata.md` — title, abstract, authorship, category, license, and arXiv form notes.
- `RESULTS-TODO.md` — results that must exist before the manuscript can be finalized.

## Rule

Do not write future experimental outcomes into the paper before they exist.

Frozen experiment artifacts remain outside this directory and must not be modified as part of paper drafting.
